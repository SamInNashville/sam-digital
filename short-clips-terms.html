SHORT-CLIPS PILOT · CUSTOMER TERMS

Clear scope for a focused first edit.

These terms apply to the one-time Sam Digital Short Clips Pilot.

In brief: The pilot is $75 paid upfront for three separate vertical MP4 clips in 9:16 format, 1080×1920. Each clip will normally run 20–60 seconds, with one consolidated revision round. Work starts only after cleared payment, source validation, and our written start acknowledgment. We target delivery three business days after that acknowledgment. The pilot is limited to the first three cleared payments.

What you receive

  • Three separate vertical MP4 clips in 9:16 format, 1080×1920.
  • Each clip will normally run 20–60 seconds.
  • Clean cuts, basic speaker reframing, a readable opening hook, and burned-in captions.
  • One consolidated revision round.

Price and timing

  • Pilot price: $75 USD, paid upfront.
  • Work begins only after cleared payment, source validation, and our written start acknowledgment.
  • Target delivery: three business days after that acknowledgment. This is a target, not a guaranteed deadline; if a material source problem changes it, we will say so before editing begins or offer a refund.
  • If you need the clips for a specific event or date, that date is not accepted unless we confirm it in writing before checkout.
  • The pilot is limited to the first three cleared payments.

Source limits

  • One source recording, up to 60 minutes.
  • Provide a customer-controlled download link or a public URL you own or are authorized to use.
  • You confirm that you own or control every needed right in the source, including guest/participant permission, music, logos, footage, and any confidential material.
  • We may request evidence or decline material when authorization is unclear. Do not send passwords, private credentials, illegal material, or content involving people who did not consent to its intended use.

Revision

One revision means one consolidated list of reasonable changes to caption text, cut points, opening hook text, or basic reframing. Send that list within five business days after review delivery. A different source, new campaign, wholesale creative redirection, or additional clips is outside the pilot and requires a new agreement. We target final delivery within one business day after receiving an in-scope revision list.

Review and delivery

Review copies may be watermarked or reduced-resolution. Clean final files are delivered after the included revision is resolved or the first proofs are accepted. If no revision or acceptance arrives within five business days after review delivery, we will close the order as accepted for fulfillment purposes and send the clean finals; this never grants us portfolio rights.

Use sam-in-nashville@pm.me for intake and order communication. The customer provides a controlled source-download link plus primary and backup private upload destinations they control for review and final files. We do not require a public source or delivery link, and customer media will not be placed in a public portfolio or repository. If the primary destination fails, we use the verified backup. If both fail, the delivery target pauses when we report the access error and restarts only after a replacement private destination is verified.

Refunds and remedies

  • Full refund before our written start acknowledgment if we cannot accept the order or the source fails validation and no workable replacement is provided.
  • “Editing begins” means our written start acknowledgment has been sent after source validation.
  • “First proofs delivered” means all three review files have been sent to the agreed contact/destination and the delivery message has not bounced or returned an access error.
  • After first proofs are delivered, the normal remedy is correction within the agreed scope rather than an automatic refund.
  • If we cause a material delay after work starts, we will disclose it and offer an attainable revised date. If that date defeats the stated use, we will review cancellation or another written remedy with you before continuing.
  • A customer cancellation before our written start acknowledgment receives the pre-start refund above. After start, cancellation does not create an automatic refund; we will give a written disposition based on completed work, the cause, and these terms. If we cannot lawfully establish source authorization before start, we cancel and refund rather than edit disputed material.
  • Nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be limited.

Privacy and retention

Source and working files are stored only in the order's access-limited local workspace and used only to fulfill the order.

They are deleted from the working system no later than seven days after accepted delivery; if the order ends without accepted delivery, no later than seven days after its final cancellation, refund, or dispute disposition; or within one business day of an earlier valid deletion request. A documented rights, privacy, payment, or legal hold may temporarily preserve only the affected evidence. This pilot does not offer extended media retention. We retain only the minimum payment, consent, delivery, incident, and accounting record—not the working media itself.

If we discover an accidental exposure or misdelivery, we stop work, revoke any link we control, notify the affected customer, and document the response.

We do not publish your source or finished clips as portfolio work without separate, explicit permission.

Access failure and support

  • If both customer-controlled delivery destinations fail, we will report the problem and request a replacement. After one reminder and five business days without a usable destination, we may close the order as destination unavailable. We will not call the files delivered or accepted, and the cancellation/closure deletion timetable applies.
  • Report playback, corruption, wrong-export, or agreed-content defects within seven calendar days after verified final delivery so we can assess them while the working media is still retained. Confirmed defects are corrected without consuming the creative revision. Later reports are still reviewed, but deleted working media may make correction new work. Privacy, rights, payment, and legal concerns are not limited by this defect-reporting window.

AI disclosure

The workflow may use local AI-assisted transcription and editing tools. A human-readable quality review is performed before delivery. No guarantee is made about views, engagement, leads, sales, or virality.

Acceptance

Payment confirms acceptance of these terms, but the order does not enter production until source validation and our written start acknowledgment. Questions or requested exceptions must be resolved in writing before payment.
