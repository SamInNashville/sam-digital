SHORT-CLIPS PILOT · CUSTOMER TERMS

Clear scope for a focused first edit.

These terms apply to the one-time Sam Digital Short Clips Pilot.

In brief: The pilot is $75 paid upfront for three captioned vertical clips up to 30 seconds each, with one consolidated revision round. We target delivery within five business days after complete materials are received and payment has cleared.

What you receive

  • Three separate vertical MP4 clips in 9:16 format.
  • Each clip is up to 30 seconds.
  • Clean cuts, basic speaker reframing, a readable opening hook, and burned-in captions.
  • One consolidated revision round.

Price and timing

  • Pilot price: $75 USD, paid upfront as a one-time payment.
  • Work begins after payment has cleared, complete materials have been received, the source has been validated, and we send a written start acknowledgment.
  • Target delivery is within five business days after complete materials are received and payment has cleared. This is a target, not a guaranteed deadline. If a material source problem changes the timing, we will say so before editing begins or offer the applicable pre-start refund described below.
  • A date for a specific event or use is not accepted unless we confirm it in writing before checkout.

Source limits and authorization

  • Provide one source recording and a customer-controlled download link or a public URL you own or are authorized to use.
  • You confirm that you own or control every needed right in the source, including guest or participant permission, music, logos, footage, and confidential material.
  • We may request evidence or decline material when authorization is unclear. Do not send passwords, private credentials, illegal material, or content involving people who did not consent to its intended use.

Revision

One revision means one consolidated list of reasonable changes to caption text, cut points, opening hook text, or basic reframing. A different source, new campaign, wholesale creative redirection, or additional clips is outside the pilot and requires a new agreement. We target final delivery within one business day after receiving an in-scope revision list.

Review and delivery

Review copies may be watermarked or reduced-resolution. Clean final files are delivered after the included revision is resolved or the first proofs are accepted. If no revision or acceptance arrives within five business days after review delivery, we will close the order as accepted for fulfillment purposes and send the clean finals; this never grants us portfolio rights.

Use sam-in-nashville@pm.me for intake and order communication. Provide a customer-controlled private destination for review and final files. We do not require a public source or delivery link, and customer media will not be placed in a public portfolio or repository.

Refunds and cancellation

  • We will issue a full refund before our written start acknowledgment if we cannot accept the order or the source fails validation and no workable replacement is provided.
  • “Editing begins” means our written start acknowledgment has been sent after source validation.
  • After editing begins, cancellation does not create an automatic refund. We will give a written disposition based on completed work, the cause, and these terms.
  • If we cannot lawfully establish source authorization before editing begins, we cancel and refund rather than edit disputed material.
  • After first proofs are delivered, the normal remedy is correction within the agreed scope rather than an automatic refund.
  • If we cause a material delay after work starts, we will disclose it and offer an attainable revised date. If that date defeats the stated use, we will review cancellation or another written remedy with you before continuing.
  • Nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be limited.

Privacy and retention

Source and working files are stored only in the order’s access-limited local workspace and used only to fulfill the order. They are deleted from the working system no later than seven days after accepted delivery, or no later than seven days after a final cancellation, refund, or dispute disposition if the order ends without accepted delivery. An earlier valid deletion request is handled within one business day. A documented rights, privacy, payment, or legal hold may temporarily preserve only the affected evidence. We retain the minimum payment, consent, delivery, incident, and accounting record—not the working media itself.

We do not publish your source or finished clips as portfolio work without separate, explicit permission.

Access failure and support

If a customer-controlled delivery destination fails, we will report the problem and request a replacement. After one reminder and five business days without a usable destination, we may close the order as destination unavailable. We will not call the files delivered or accepted, and the cancellation or closure deletion timetable applies.

Report playback, corruption, wrong-export, or agreed-content defects within seven calendar days after verified final delivery so we can assess them while the working media is still retained. Confirmed defects are corrected without consuming the creative revision. Privacy, rights, payment, and legal concerns are not limited by this defect-reporting window.

AI disclosure and acceptance

The workflow may use local AI-assisted transcription and editing tools. A human-readable quality review is performed before delivery. No guarantee is made about views, engagement, leads, sales, or virality.

Payment confirms acceptance of these terms, but the order does not enter production until complete materials are received, source validation is complete, and our written start acknowledgment is sent. Questions or requested exceptions must be resolved in writing before payment.
